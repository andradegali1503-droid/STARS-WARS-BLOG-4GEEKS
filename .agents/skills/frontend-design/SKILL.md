# Frontend Design Skill: Star Wars Galactic Database

## Visual Identity & Atmosphere
- **Color Palette**:
  - Deep Space Black: `#050505`
  - Secondary Void: `#101010`
  - Sith/Subtle Dark Glass: `rgba(16, 16, 16, 0.75)` with `backdrop-blur-md`
  - Imperial Gold / Jedi Amber: `#D8B45A`
  - Light Gold Glow: `#F5D98A`
  - Holo Blue: `#48CAE4`
  - Pure Starlight White: `#F5F5F5`
  - Starship Hull Gray: `#888888`
  - Subtle Border Golden Accent: `rgba(216, 180, 90, 0.25)`

## Typography & HUD Elements
- Star Wars aesthetic uses bold, geometric headings, tracked out uppercase labels (`tracking-widest`, `tracking-wider`).
- Tech readout metadata labels: monospace font, uppercase, golden or dimmed cyan accents (`[SEC-01]`, `ID: // SW-042`).
- Terminal-like corner brackets and subtle technical grids.

## Components & Cards
- Holographic glass cards with glowing borders on hover.
- High contrast readable text against deep dark backgrounds.
- Minimalist, high-end sci-fi interface avoiding cheap clutter.
